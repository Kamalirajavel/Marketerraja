Common Questions

ANSWERS FOR OUR CLIENTS

Simple answers to common questions asked by business owners — local and global — before launching campaigns.

How much budget do I need every day?
For local campaigns, you can start with as low as ₹300–₹500 per day. For global campaigns targeting international audiences, budgets vary by country and industry. We help you plan the right budget for your specific goals.
When will I start receiving customer phone calls?
Google Ads is incredibly fast. Your ads will go live within 24 to 48 hours of setup, and customers can call you on the exact same day.
Do I need a huge website for this?
No. We can set up a simple, high-speed 1-page business layout that is optimized perfectly to convert click traffic into fast WhatsApp messages and leads into conversations.
Will my business appear above competitors on Google?
Yes. Your ads can appear at the top of Google search results — above local competitors in your city and above global competitors in international markets.
Do Google Ads work for global brands?
Absolutely. Google Ads works for businesses of all sizes — from small local shops reaching nearby customers to global brands targeting audiences across multiple countries.
What if I do not know anything about Google Ads?
No problem. We handle the complete Google Ads setup, keyword targeting, optimization, and campaign management for you
